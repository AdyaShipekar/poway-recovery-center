About Poway Recovery Center

Care that makes room for complexity.

We believe people are more than a diagnosis, a difficult season, or a single decision.

Our approach

Trauma-informed. Person-centered. Community-rooted.

We create low-barrier pathways to support by pairing peer connection with practical education and referrals to clinical care. Our work is grounded in dignity, consent, cultural humility, and the understanding that change is rarely linear.

We do not promise a perfect journey. We promise to meet people with honesty, respect, and a focus on safety.
